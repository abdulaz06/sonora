**see your music.** sonora 1.0 turns the visualizer into a finished app.

### New
- **Home menu.** Every launch opens on the five styles. Hover to watch one move, open it to compare looks and views side by side, press Start and the thumbnail grows into the live visual. *Continue* picks up where you left off.
- **First run.** A short intro: what sonora is, an audio check that tells you when it can hear your music (and what to try when it can't), and the four words you need: style, look, view, palette.
- **Set Mode.** Let sonora choose, like a VJ. It switches style, look and view when the track changes, or on a big drop after a long build, and picks to suit the music. Press `M`, or use the menu card or the tray.
- **Now playing.** Song, artist and cover from any player that reports to Windows, for a few seconds on each track change, with previous / play / next.
- **Settings rebuilt.** Every control has an (i) card with plain explanations and pictures where they help. Palettes are shown on the current style.
- **Updates.** sonora checks GitHub on launch and offers a one-click install.
- Tray menu (Show, Menu, Set Mode, Fullscreen, Quit), fullscreen on the display you choose, output devices remembered by their Windows id, a clear message on graphics cards that can't run it.

### Install
Download **sonora_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe** (or the MSI). The installers aren't code-signed yet: if SmartScreen warns, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

### Privacy
Audio is analysed on your computer only. Nothing is uploaded; the update check is the only network request and can be turned off in Settings › System.
