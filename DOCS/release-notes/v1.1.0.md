**make videos with sonora, record it live, and fly over worlds that look real.**

### export a video from a song
- **export** (on the home menu, in the tray, or press `E`) turns an audio file into a video of sonora reacting to it: mp3, wav, flac, m4a, ogg / opus, or the sound of an mp4 or mov. artwork in the file gives album colours and the now-playing card; its title and artist fill in the text.
- take the whole track, or a part: the song strip shows the waveform, the intensity, every bar, the builds and drops, and three suggested moments. click a drop, or drag; the ends snap to bars.
- one style for the whole video, a **styles tour** that cuts on the bars (a drop always lands on a cut), a **beat switch** on the bar you pick, or a **showcase** that changes palette or look on every bar. any style, look, view and palette, album colours and your own hue included.
- 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5; 720p up to 4k; 30 or 60 fps; standard or best (rendered larger and scaled down, sharper and slower). tall videos frame the 3d views for a phone.
- the preview plays it with the song in the shape you chose, with the phone apps' safe zones shown for 9:16.
- videos are rendered frame by frame in the song's own time, so a slower pc just takes longer: the video comes out the same. a progress bar shows how long is left, and you can cancel. your pc stays usable while it works.
- hardware h.264 and windows' own aac encoder: nothing extra to install. videos go to `videos\sonora`, with an **open folder** button when they're done.
- every video carries a small sonora mark in a corner you choose (kept clear of the phone apps' buttons on 9:16). an optional 2-second end card says "made with sonora · sonoravisuals.com"; turn it off on the export screen or in settings › recording.
- please only export music you have the rights to use.

### text in your videos
- a title card with the song and artist, as a line in the corner or a centred cinematic card, on screen when you want it.
- your own lines of text, each with its own time (snapped to bars if you like), place, size, font and case. two brand fonts and four display fonts are included (instrument serif, fraunces, bebas neue, space grotesk).
- the now-playing card with the cover, as in the app.
- the preview shows the text exactly as the video will.

### record live
- **record** (`F9`, the record button, the tray, or the controller) captures the visual itself, not your desktop, with the sound sonora is hearing: for dj sets, streams and quick clips.
- picture and sound stay in sync within a frame, and recording doesn't make the visual stutter. a small indicator shows the time; sonora warns before the disk fills and stops cleanly.
- record at the size of the window or screen, or at 720p up to 4k, at 30 or 60 fps (settings › recording). same mark and optional end card as exports. recordings go to `videos\sonora`.

### voyage: new city and ocean worlds
- **city** is now a lived-in world: real aerial photos of farmland, woods, towns and shores, with trees and hedgerows standing up from the fields, mountains in rock and snow, towns of houses with pitched roofs, mid-rises and towers, streets with pavements and crossings, rivers and lakes.
- **ocean** is a real sea: rolling swells and wind waves, the sky reflected at low angles, light glowing through thin crests, foam where waves break, sun glints.
- soft shadows, ambient occlusion and haze that grows with distance, so you can feel how big the world is.
- the materials keep their natural colours; your palette now lives in the light: the sun, the sky, the haze, the city lights and the reflections. each palette gives the world its own time of day.
- the same speed as before on medium; a little more work on high.

### faster browser demo
- the live page on the website now starts much faster: styles load when you first open them.

### install
download **sonora_1.1.0_x64-setup.exe** (or the msi), or let sonora update itself if *check for updates* is on. the installers aren't code-signed yet: if smartscreen warns, choose **more info → run anyway**.

### privacy
audio, album covers and the songs you export are analysed on your computer only. nothing is uploaded; the update check is the only network request and can be turned off in settings › system.
