**richer colour, smoother ink.**

### new palettes
- nine palettes on keys 1–9, tuned to look vivid in every style, look and view: **ocean, aurora, ember, neon, crimson, solar, violet, sunset, acid**.
- ocean, ember, neon, crimson and violet look as before. aurora is reworked (teal and green with a touch of orchid), gold becomes **solar** (amber gold that stays golden), and **sunset** and **acid** are new.
- the two-tone palettes are gone. if you used one, sonora switches you to the closest palette (gold → solar, the ember / crimson ones → ember / crimson, cold and ivory → ocean).
- **voyage** now takes the palette's colour across the whole fractal, its sky and its sea, not only in the glowing details.
- **wisp** keeps its smoke trails coloured instead of fading to grey where they pile up.
- **custom** colours and **colour drift** build their palettes the same way, never go muddy or grey, and palette changes fade without passing through grey.
- each palette swatch now shows its main colour and, in the corner, its accent.

### smoother ink
- ink drops grow into the flow instead of popping in, fine lines in marble no longer break up, and ink looks the same at any refresh rate (60, 120, 165 hz…).

### frame rate
- **settings › performance › frame rate** now defaults to *match display*, so a 144 or 165 hz screen gets every frame. half and third of the display rate are offered too, and rates that don't divide your display are marked.
- **show fps** (or the `P` key) puts a small counter in a corner: frames per second, the target and the slowest frame. it is never part of the visual itself.

### install
download **sonora_1.0.1_x64-setup.exe** (or the msi), or let sonora update itself if *check for updates* is on. the installers aren't code-signed yet: if smartscreen warns, choose **more info → run anyway**.

### privacy
audio is analysed on your computer only. nothing is uploaded; the update check is the only network request and can be turned off in settings › system.
