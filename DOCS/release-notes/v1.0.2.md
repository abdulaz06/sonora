**your album's colours, and a remote for your tv.**

### colours from your album art
- sonora can now paint with the colours of the cover of what's playing. when the track changes, the new album's colours fade in over a couple of seconds, together with the now-playing toast. the next song from the same album keeps them.
- the colours are picked the same way as the palettes, so they stay vivid in every style, look and view: never muddy, grey or blown out. paper looks (sumi) stay on bone paper.
- no cover, an advert, a podcast without art, or a black-and-white cover: your own palette stays.
- the **album** tile comes first in every palette picker, with the current cover. the `A` key turns album colours on and off; picking a palette by hand (or `1`–`9`) turns them off. colour drift pauses while a cover chooses the colours, and set mode leaves the colours to the cover.
- on for new installs. if you're updating, your palette stays as it is and a small note offers album colours once.
- the cover is read and analysed on your computer only. it never leaves it.
- on the website's live page, a file you play shows its embedded cover (mp3, m4a, flac) and uses its colours too.

### the controller (second screen)
- for tv setups: the visual stays full screen on the tv, and a controller window on your laptop works as the remote.
- a live preview of exactly what the tv shows, now playing with its controls, set mode (on and off, next now, lock to the current style, how often it may switch, what it can choose, and a calm ↔ driving slider), styles, looks and views, palettes with album, responsiveness, a new **glow** slider, camera presets, which display shows the visual, **blackout** and **freeze**, and every setting.
- open it with `R`, from settings › set mode, or from the tray. the first time the visual goes full screen with a second display connected, sonora offers it on that display.
- keys pressed in the controller act on the visual. closing the controller never stops the visual, and if the tv is unplugged, the visual comes back to a screen that's there.

### neon's colour drift
- with colour drift on, neon now sways gently around its colours instead of turning all the way round, where its magenta, cyan and yellow went olive or dull in some styles.

### install
download **sonora_1.0.2_x64-setup.exe** (or the msi), or let sonora update itself if *check for updates* is on. the installers aren't code-signed yet: if smartscreen warns, choose **more info → run anyway**.

### privacy
audio and album covers are analysed on your computer only. nothing is uploaded; the update check is the only network request and can be turned off in settings › system.
