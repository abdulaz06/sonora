# Third-party assets

Assets that ship with sonora (the app and the website) and come from outside
the project. Code dependencies and their licences are listed in
`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` (generated by `scripts/third-party-notices.mjs`).

Nothing here uses a restrictive licence: every texture and image is CC0 or in
the public domain; fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the texture
transcoder is Apache-2.0.

## Voyage City textures (`src/assets/voyage/*.ktx2`)

Built by `scripts/textures/prep.py` and `scripts/textures/build.mjs` from the
sources below (downloaded into the git-ignored `local-media/textures-src/`),
resized to 1024², and encoded as KTX2 (Basis Universal: UASTC + Zstandard for
the aerial imagery, ETC1S for the tiled materials) with full mip chains. The
aerial images also carry a vegetation-height channel derived from the image
itself (trees and hedgerows: green, rough, not bright).

### Aerial imagery (USDA NAIP via the USGS National Map)

National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP) orthoimagery, USDA Farm Service
Agency, served by the USGS National Map (`USGSNAIPImagery` ImageServer). US
federal government imagery: public domain. Each tile is an `exportImage`
request (1024 × 1024 JPEG, Web Mercator) for the WGS84 bounding box given:

| File | Place | Bounding box (lon/lat) |
|---|---|---|
| `a-farm1.ktx2` | farmland, Lancaster County, PA | −76.080, 39.980, −76.045, 40.007 |
| `a-farm2.ktx2` | farmland, Lancaster County, PA | −76.040, 40.040, −76.005, 40.067 |
| `a-farm3.ktx2` | farmland and a small town, Lancaster County, PA | −76.215, 40.020, −76.180, 40.047 |
| `a-shore.ktx2` | salt marsh and coast, Cape Cod, MA | −70.005, 41.890, −69.975, 41.912 |
| `a-forest.ktx2` | mixed forest, Green Mountains, VT | −72.860, 43.975, −72.825, 44.002 |
| `a-town.ktx2` | rowhouse rooftops, Philadelphia, PA | −75.172, 39.930, −75.160, 39.939 |

Service: https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPImagery/ImageServer
(e.g. `…/exportImage?bbox=-76.080,39.980,-76.045,40.007&bboxSR=4326&imageSR=3857&size=1024,1024&format=jpg&f=image`).
Program: https://www.fsa.usda.gov/programs-and-services/aerial-photography/imagery-programs/naip-imagery/

## Texture transcoder (`src/styles/voyage/basis/`)

Basis Universal transcoder (`basis_transcoder.mjs`, `.wasm`), © Binomial LLC,
Apache License 2.0 (https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal), as shipped
with three.js r187 (`examples/jsm/libs/basis`, MIT-licensed package), with an ES
module export line added (`NOTICE.md` there). The build-time encoder is the
`ktx2-encoder` npm package (MIT), which bundles the Basis Universal encoder
(Apache-2.0); it is a development dependency and doesn't ship.
